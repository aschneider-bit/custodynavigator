Parenting Plan

This parenting plan is entered into by Alex Chen ("Parent A") and Jordan Chen ("Parent B") for the benefit of their minor child, Mia Chen, born March 14, 2019.

I. Legal Custody

The parties shall have joint legal custody of the minor child. Both parents share the right and responsibility to make major decisions regarding the child's education, health care, and general welfare. Neither parent shall make major decisions unilaterally without the agreement of the other parent.

II. Parenting Time — 2-2-3 Rotating Schedule

Parent A (Alex) shall have parenting time: Monday and Tuesday in Week 1; Friday, Saturday, and Sunday in Week 1; Wednesday and Thursday in Week 2 (alternating).

Parent B (Jordan) shall have parenting time: Wednesday and Thursday in Week 1; Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday in Week 2; Friday, Saturday, and Sunday in Week 2 (alternating).

This schedule results in approximately 50% parenting time for each parent.

III. Exchanges

Exchanges shall occur at the child's school (Anoka Elementary) via school drop-off and pick-up. Weekend exchanges shall occur at 6:00 p.m. at the residence of the receiving parent.

IV. Holidays and School Breaks

In odd-numbered years: Parent A (Alex) has the child for Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve (Dec 24), and Spring Break. Parent B (Jordan) has the child for Christmas Day (Dec 25) and New Year's.

In even-numbered years: the holiday schedule reverses.

Summer break shall be divided equally between the parents, with each parent having no less than two consecutive weeks.

V. Parent-Child Contact

Each parent may contact the child by telephone or video call daily when the child is with the other parent, between 6:00 p.m. and 8:00 p.m. Neither parent shall interfere with this contact.

VI. Education

Both parents shall have access to all educational records. Both parents are encouraged to attend school events and parent-teacher conferences. Either parent may communicate with school staff regarding the child's educational needs.

VII. Medical Care

The minor child shall be covered under Parent B's (Jordan's) health insurance. Both parents shall be notified of medical appointments. Routine medical care may be obtained by the parent who has parenting time. Non-emergency medical procedures require the agreement of both parents.

VIII. Child Support

Child support shall be calculated pursuant to Minnesota Statutes § 518A. Extraordinary expenses, including unreimbursed medical expenses, childcare, and educational expenses, shall be shared equally by both parents.
