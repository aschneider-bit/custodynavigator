1. Custody Navigator Best free custody schedule builder

If you're still figuring out what your custody schedule should look like, start here. Custody Navigator lets you browse and customize templates (2-2-3, week-on/week-off, and more), see exactly how each schedule plays out on a calendar, and calculate parenting time percentages before you commit. It's free to use and built specifically for the schedule-building phase, not a full co-parenting platform, but the best tool for that first, critical decision. You can integrate it with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or your preferred calendar app so you don't have to manually create the schedule.

2. OurFamilyWizard Best co-parenting platform

OurFamilyWizard is the most widely court-recognized co-parenting platform on the market. It combines a shared calendar, secure messaging, expense tracking, and an information bank in one place. Courts and attorneys in all 50 states accept its records. The platform has added AI-assisted message tone tools to help keep communication child-focused.

3. Custody X Change

Custody X Change generates legally formatted parenting plan documents alongside its scheduling calendar, messaging, and expense tracking.

4. TalkingParents

TalkingParents records all co-parent communication in tamper-proof logs from day one. Every message, call, and exchange is timestamped and unalterable, accepted in all 50 states. It also includes a shared calendar, expense tracking, and a writing assist tool that flags messages before you send them.

5. AppClose

AppClose goes further than most: secure messaging, audio/video calling with optional recording, GPS check-in at exchanges, and a built-in payment system. Its AI "Co-Parent Assist" reviews drafts for tone before you send. Comes with a 60-day free trial and free access for domestic violence survivors and active military.

6. 2houses

2houses covers the core co-parenting toolkit: shared calendar, messaging, expense tracking, a family info bank, and a shared journal for photos and updates from both homes.

7. Google Calendar

Not a dedicated co-parenting tool, but for parents who communicate well and just need a shared calendar, Google Calendar works fine. Create a shared calendar, color-code each parent's days, and sync it to any device.

8. Cozi

Cozi is a free family organizer built around a shared calendar, shopping lists, and meal planning. It's not designed for co-parenting, but its color-coded calendar and automatic notifications make it easy for both households to stay on the same page. A premium tier, Cozi Gold, adds extra features for families who want more.

9. FamilyWall

FamilyWall combines a shared calendar with a private family feed, to-do lists, and messaging. Like Cozi, it's a general family organizer rather than a co-parenting tool, but it works well for low-conflict situations where you just need a simple, shared view of the schedule. Free to use with a premium tier available.

How to choose

Custody schedule builder

If you're still figuring out what schedule works for your family, start here. A custody schedule builder lets you visualize different arrangements, calculate parenting time percentages, and land on a plan before you commit to anything. This is the step most parents skip, and regret. Best pick: Custody Navigator (free).

Co-parenting platform

Once you have a schedule, a co-parenting platform gives you a shared space to manage communication, track expenses, and coordinate logistics. These tools are built specifically for separated parents, with features like secure messaging and tamper-proof records that general calendar apps don't offer. If your situation involves conflict or legal proceedings, this is the category you need. Best pick: OurFamilyWizard.

Calendar app

If you already have a parenting plan in place and communicate well with your co-parent, a shared calendar app may be all you need. Google Calendar, Cozi, and FamilyWall all offer free shared calendars that sync across devices. None are built for co-parenting specifically, but for low-conflict families they get the job done without a subscription. Best pick: Based on your personal preference.
