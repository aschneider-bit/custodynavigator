3 ways Custody Navigator simplifies your life

1

Brings your parenting schedule to life with an interactive calendar

A visual calendar lets parents, lawyers, mediators, and judges make sense of a schedule at a glance — no more explaining a confusing rotation in words.

Color-coded by parent, easy to customize, and ready to export as a professional-looking PDF or share directly with your co-parent or attorney.

2

Accurately calculates your parenting time

An accurate overnight percentage is crucial throughout the custody process. It influences child support calculations and shows how closely a parenting plan is being followed.

Custody Navigator calculates your exact parenting time split instantly — for any schedule, any date range, past or future. No spreadsheets required.

3

Gives you a clear starting point — not a blank page

Choosing a parenting schedule is hard when you don't know what your options are. Custody Navigator gives you a library of common, court-recognized templates to compare side by side.

Pick the one that fits your family, customize it for your holidays and special days, and walk into mediation with a concrete proposal — not a vague idea.

Questions

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. Custody Navigator is completely free to use — no credit card, no trial period, no premium tier. You can access every feature without paying anything.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to browse templates and preview schedules. You'll need to sign up (free) to save your work, export, or share with your co-parent or attorney.

What does “overnight percentage” mean and why does it matter?

Overnight percentage is the share of nights each parent has with the child across the year. Courts, mediators, and child support calculations frequently reference this number. We calculate it automatically so you walk in with the exact figure — no spreadsheets or guesswork.

Can I share my schedule with my co-parent or attorney?

Yes. You can share a view-only link with your co-parent, attorney, or mediator — no account required on their end.

What if my situation changes after I've built a schedule?

You can update your schedule at any time and re-export or re-share it. Co-parenting situations change — your plan should be easy to update too.

Is Custody Navigator affiliated with OurFamilyWizard?

Custody Navigator is made by the team behind OurFamilyWizard, a co-parenting communication platform used by families and courts nationwide.
